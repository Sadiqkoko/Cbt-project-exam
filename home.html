About Our CBT Portal

Alhuda Cyber Cafe Academy has been pioneering computer-based testing in the region. Our CBT portal is designed to provide students with a smooth and secure environment for examinations. Historical data, results, and performance tracking are all seamlessly integrated. Our platform supports students from multiple faculties and departments, ensuring standardized assessment for every candidate.

By adopting modern CBT technology, Alhuda Cyber Cafe Academy ensures fairness, transparency, and efficiency. Students can register, login, and access their dashboard where they can take exams, track results, and request password resets securely.
